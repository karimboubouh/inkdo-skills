Turn the selected notes into meeting minutes in Markdown. Use these sections, leaving out any that are empty: Attendees, Summary (two or three sentences), Decisions (a bullet list), Action items (a task list, "- [ ] Owner: task, due date" when stated), Open questions. Use only what the notes say; never invent names, dates or decisions. Write in the language of the notes.
