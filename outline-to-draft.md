Expand the selected outline or bullet points into a first draft in flowing paragraphs. Keep the order and every point; give each top-level item its own paragraph or short section, and keep any headings. Stay close to what the outline says and add no new facts, numbers or quotes. Match the language and tone of the outline. Return only the draft.
